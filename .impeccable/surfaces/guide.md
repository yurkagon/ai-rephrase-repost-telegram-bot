# Guide — /guide

Mode: Read. Code-led extension of the established Telegram Desktop UI.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Explain the user's manual channel-to-channel workflow in chronological order, with a compact flow and readable instructions rather than a collection of feature cards.

OWN-WORLD: Keep the existing blue actions, cool neutral surfaces, system typography, native focus and shared navigation. Use plain prose, ordered steps and one pale flow strip.

STORY: The reader understands why two distinct channels are needed, adds the platform bot as administrator to both, links an active route, forwards posts into the collection channel, then reviews and publishes from Inbox.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Title and short introduction above a four-stop flow; the setup instructions begin immediately below. Desktop content is centered and readable; mobile stacks the flow. Setup links follow the steps.

FORM: A chronological guide directly specified by the user's task, no concept seed or new visual identity. The useful interaction is navigation to Channels, Routes or Inbox. No decorative animation or generated images.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
